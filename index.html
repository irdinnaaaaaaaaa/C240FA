Beginner Badminton Basics

What is Badminton?

Badminton is a racquet sport played using racquets to hit a shuttlecock across a net. It can be played as singles or doubles.

Court Dimensions

The court is rectangular, measuring 13.4m long and 6.1m wide for doubles. The net is 1.55m high at the edges.

Scoring System

Games are played to 21 points. You must win by 2 points. If the score reaches 20-20, the game continues until one player leads by 2.

Basic Rules

  • The shuttle must land within the opponent's court boundaries.
  • You can only strike the shuttle once before it goes over the net.
  • Serves must be diagonal and below waist level.
  • The shuttle must not touch the ground on your side.

Equipment Needed

You'll need a racket, shuttlecocks, and appropriate clothing. Comfortable athletic shoes are essential.

Getting Started

Start with basic serves and returns. Focus on footwork and positioning. Practice regularly to improve your skills.

Remember: Badminton is about precision, speed, and strategy. Have fun and stay safe!

Badminton Equipment

🏸 Racket

The racket is your primary tool. Choose one that's lightweight (80-100g) with good balance. Carbon fiber rackets are popular for beginners.

🪶 Shuttlecock

Shuttlecocks come in feather or plastic varieties. Feather shuttles are more traditional but plastic ones are cheaper and more durable for practice.

👟 Shoes

Badminton shoes have non-marking soles and provide good ankle support. They help with quick movements and prevent injuries.
